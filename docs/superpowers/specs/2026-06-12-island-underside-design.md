# Hero island underside — jagged rock + crystal keel (2026-06-12)

## Problem

The floating island's underside was a single smooth inverted cone — it read
as a spinning top, not a mountain chunk torn from the earth.

## Design (user-approved)

Replace the cone inside the hero `peak` group with:

- **Three faceted inverted cones** (6/5 radial segments so the facets read
  as rock): a large earth main mass plus two smaller tilted shards (earth
  and slate) shouldering out the sides. Rims tuck under the green collar;
  lawn, collar, machine, shadow untouched.
- **Crystal keel**: a small gold octahedron floating in the air gap below
  the lowest tip — slow spin, faint pulse (quieter than the machine core),
  riding the island bob phase so the gap stays constant.
- **Debris**: three tiny earth/slate octahedron bits hovering around the
  underside, each bobbing on its own phase with a lazy spin.
